## English

### Short description (260 characters)

Risk it, little biscuit! A charred cookie wants to burn the whole kitchen to ash. Crunch through hordes of sugar-crazed snacks, pick one of nine food heroes, evolve absurd weapons and wrest the ember shards from four rulers gone mad. A bullet-heaven roguelite.

### About this game

```
[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/horde.png[/img]

[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/banner_01_keks_en.png[/img]
Cookies get eaten. Unless they fight back.

Ever since the Big Ones left, the kitchen belongs to the snacks. But deep beneath it all, inside the volcano, the [b]Great Oven[/b] is glowing. A hundred years ago, one cookie slipped behind the tray and nobody ever took it out. Now he is [b]the Charred One[/b], and he's stoking the oven until everything is as black as he is.

He broke his glowing heart into four [b]ember shards[/b] and handed them to four rulers. They've been losing it ever since.

Only one old kitchen timer is still ticking: [b]Pomo[/b], half tomato, half egg timer. She wakes the bravest crumb she can find. You.
```
